# Attribution and publishing guidance

## Simulation assets

The model under `franka_emika_panda/` comes from MuJoCo Menagerie's Franka Panda model, with modifications for the CMU Robot Autonomy coursework. Preserve `franka_emika_panda/LICENSE` (Apache-2.0) and the model's original `README.md`. Attribution for the original Franka description appears in that README.

## Educational starter files

Some Python modules originated as coursework starter templates and retain their explanatory comments. The present packaging does not transfer ownership of those templates or purport to relicense them. The student's additions to those templates are distinguishable from supplied scaffolding only when reviewed against the original course distribution.

Do not assume course starter files or full assignment solutions may be published publicly merely because they can be uploaded to GitHub. Confirm your instructor/course academic-integrity and redistribution policies first. Until then, use a **private** repository.

## License for original additions

No root-level software license has been asserted because the project mixes user code, course-supplied materials, and separately licensed assets. You may add a license to your independently authored portions after confirming the rights for the other components.
